# High Availability & Failover

High Availability (HA) ensures a prearranged level of operational performance during peak times and node outages.

## Metrics
- **MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures):** Expected time between operational interruptions.
- **MTTR (Mean Time to Repair):** Average time taken to repair and recover a failed component.
- **The Nines:** 99.9% (8.76 hours/yr downtime) vs 99.999% (5.26 minutes/yr downtime).
